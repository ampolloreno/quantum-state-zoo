A field guide to quantum information

Classical probability describes information by probability distributions. Quantum theory adds states that can interfere, measurements that cannot always be reconciled, and multipartite correlations without a classical counterpart. The most useful way to see these differences is through explicit, small examples.

Each specimen below states what it is, what operational task reveals its behavior, and how small the construction can be. Minimality is always relative to a particular task and size measure. Source links lead to original papers or related references.

Editorial status. This is a starter catalogue under scholarly review. Some source links are secondary rather than original publications; some dimension claims are scoped to a task or family. Contributions and corrected citations are welcome.

Help improve the Zoo

The purpose of this site is to be a useful, corrigible reference, not a collection of unqualified superlatives. A proposed entry should include a succinct mathematical definition, the operational property, the classical or restricted benchmark, a small witness, and a justified size statement.
